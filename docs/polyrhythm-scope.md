# Polyrhythm — scope, cultural care and music licensing

Complements `editorial-guidelines.md` (which stays binding for voice, evidence, formats and banned patterns).

## Genre families
| Family (`genre`) | Includes | Accent / motif |
|---|---|---|
| `jazz` | All eras: early jazz, swing, bebop, cool, modal, spiritual, ECM, contemporary | brass `#e0aa45`, score bars |
| `funk` | Funk, soul, R&B, soul jazz, boogie | orange `#ef6a3a`, syncopated bass line |
| `afrobeat` | Afrobeat, highlife, Ivorian and Ghanaian scenes, Afro-funk, Ethio-jazz, contemporary Afrobeats | green `#5fbf6e`, 12-pulse interlocking grid |
| `latin` | Afro-Cuban, salsa, Brazilian (samba, bossa nova, MPB), reggae, zouk, kompa | teal `#3fb8a9`, 3-2 son clave |

Motifs are abstract rhythm diagrams, not cultural ornaments: never add generic "African", "tropical" or similar
decoration. Any place- or era-specific visual reference must match the work discussed.

## Cultural care (adapted from the Cadence Afrique charter)
- Name the country, city, period, language and community precisely. Never treat Africa, Latin America or the
  Caribbean as one homogeneous culture.
- Do not reduce a tradition to a single origin when it results from multiple circulations; show the circulation.
- Avoid "the first", "the inventor", "the father of" without solid, preferably independent sources.
- Check names, accents and diacritics (e.g. Fela Aníkúlápó Kútì, Ernesto Djédjé), and credit women and collectives
  when sources identify their role.
- An artist's statement proves the statement was made, not that a disputed fact is established; use a second
  independent source for contested or important claims, and state disagreements honestly.
- Do not confuse popularity, artistic value and quality.

## Music licensing
- Allowed in clips: tracks from free libraries whose licence explicitly allows use in social-media videos, and the
  owner's own productions. Each track is listed in `audio/library.json` with source URL, licence name and URL,
  retrieval date, saved licence evidence, and attribution text (or null).
- Prefer licences without attribution (Pixabay Content License, Mixkit License). Creative Commons: only CC0 or CC BY
  (no NC, no ND); CC BY credit is posted as the first reply.
- Never: excerpts of commercial recordings, "no copyright" uploads on YouTube or similar, tracks whose licence is
  unclear, or anything that imitates a real artist's recording or voice.
- Platform catalogue songs (owner decision, 2026-10-16): the real recording may be used **only** through the
  platform's own licensed catalogue: TikTok sounds attached by id (`tiktokMusicId`), Instagram/Facebook songs
  added by the owner with the in-app music sticker. We never download, cut or upload the recording ourselves;
  rendered files keep a library track, which is also the only audio on X.
- A library track illustrates mood only: never imply it is the music being discussed (alt text and on-screen
  text must not suggest so).
- If a track ever receives a claim, mark it `blocked` in the library with the date and reason.
